1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This policy sets out the minimum information security controls applicable to
all employees, contractors and third parties who access company systems or
handle company data.

1.2 It applies to all environments, including production, staging, development
and any personal device enrolled in the mobile device management programme.

1.3 Breach of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
dismissal, and for third parties, termination of the engagement.

2. ACCESS CONTROL

2.1 Access is granted on the principle of least privilege. Each request must be
approved by the data owner and is reviewed quarterly.

2.2 Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access, all
administrative accounts, and any system holding personal data.

2.3 Shared accounts are prohibited except where a documented technical
limitation exists and a compensating control has been approved by the Chief
Information Security Officer.

2.4 Accounts of departing staff must be disabled within four (4) hours of the
termination of employment being confirmed to IT.

3. PASSWORD AND CREDENTIAL STANDARDS

3.1 Passwords must be at least fourteen (14) characters. Complexity rules are
not imposed; length and uniqueness are preferred.

3.2 Credentials must never be transmitted over email, chat or ticketing systems.
The approved secrets manager is the only permitted channel.

3.3 Service account credentials must be rotated at least every one hundred and
eighty (180) days, and immediately on suspicion of compromise.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION

4.1 All data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
Where classification is unclear, data is treated as Confidential until the data
owner determines otherwise.

4.2 Restricted data may not leave approved systems and may not be copied to
removable media under any circumstances.

4.3 Confidential and Restricted data at rest must be encrypted using AES-256 or
an equivalent approved algorithm. Data in transit must use TLS 1.2 or above.

5. INCIDENT RESPONSE

5.1 All suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team
within one (1) hour of discovery, regardless of the hour or day.

5.2 The security team triages every report within two (2) hours and assigns a
severity between 1 and 4.

5.3 A Severity 1 incident triggers the major incident process, including
notification of the executive team within thirty (30) minutes of classification.

5.4 Where an incident involves personal data, the Data Protection Officer
assesses notifiability and, where required, notifies the supervisory authority
within seventy-two (72) hours of the organisation becoming aware.

5.5 A written post-incident review is produced within ten (10) business days for
every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident.

6. VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT

6.1 Internet-facing systems are scanned weekly. Internal systems are scanned
monthly.

6.2 Critical vulnerabilities must be remediated within seven (7) days, high
within thirty (30) days, medium within ninety (90) days.

7. BUSINESS CONTINUITY

7.1 Backups of all production data are taken daily and retained for thirty-five
(35) days, with a monthly backup retained for thirteen (13) months.

7.2 Restore testing is performed at least quarterly. A restore test that fails
is treated as a Severity 2 incident.

7.3 The recovery time objective for tier-one systems is four (4) hours and the
recovery point objective is fifteen (15) minutes.

8. THIRD PARTY AND SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT

8.1 Every supplier with access to Confidential or Restricted data must complete
a security assessment before contract signature and annually thereafter.

8.2 Contracts with such suppliers must include a right to audit, a breach
notification obligation of no more than twenty-four (24) hours, and a
requirement to delete or return data on termination.

9. TRAINING AND AWARENESS

9.1 All staff complete security awareness training on joining and annually
thereafter. Completion rates are reported to the audit committee.

9.2 Simulated phishing exercises are run at least six (6) times per year. Staff
failing three exercises in a rolling twelve month period are enrolled in
additional training.

10. POLICY REVIEW

10.1 This policy is reviewed annually, or sooner following a material incident
or a significant change in the regulatory environment.

10.2 Revision 6 was approved by the board on 11 November 2024 and supersedes
Revision 5 in its entirety.
