MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 14 March 2024
(the "Effective Date") by and between Northwind Analytics Ltd., a company
registered in England and Wales with company number 09488213, having its
registered office at 42 Bishopsgate, London EC2N 4AJ ("Supplier"), and
Cedarpoint Financial Group Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place
of business at 1900 Market Street, Philadelphia, PA 19103 ("Client").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the data analytics and reporting services described in
Schedule A, as amended from time to time by written agreement of the parties.

1.2 "Deliverables" means the reports, dashboards, models and documentation
produced by Supplier in the course of performing the Services.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one
party to the other, whether orally, in writing or in any other form, that is
designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of
disclosure.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial
term of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for
successive periods of twelve (12) months each (each a "Renewal Term") unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days
before the end of the then-current term.
===PAGE===
3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Client shall pay Supplier an annual subscription fee of USD 480,000, payable
in four equal quarterly instalments of USD 120,000 in advance.

3.2 Supplier may increase the subscription fee once per Renewal Term by no more
than the lesser of (a) four percent (4%) or (b) the increase in the Consumer
Price Index over the preceding twelve months.

3.3 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt. Overdue amounts
accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate
permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower.

3.4 Fee schedule by service tier:

  Tier          Included Seats    Annual Fee (USD)    Support Response
  Standard      25                180,000             next business day
  Professional  100               480,000             four hours
  Enterprise    unlimited         960,000             one hour

4. SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 Supplier shall maintain availability of the hosted Services of at least
99.5% measured monthly, excluding Scheduled Maintenance.

4.2 If availability falls below 99.5% in any calendar month, Client is entitled
to a service credit equal to five percent (5%) of the monthly fee for each full
percentage point below the target, capped at thirty percent (30%) of the monthly
fee.

4.3 Scheduled Maintenance shall not exceed eight (8) hours per calendar month
and shall be notified to Client at least five (5) business days in advance.
===PAGE===
5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information in strict
confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party except to its employees,
advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for the purposes of this
Agreement.

5.2 The obligations in this Section 5 survive termination of this Agreement for
a period of five (5) years.

6. DATA PROTECTION

6.1 Supplier acts as a processor and Client as a controller in respect of
personal data processed under this Agreement, within the meaning of the UK GDPR.

6.2 Supplier shall not transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom or the
European Economic Area without Client's prior written consent.

6.3 Supplier shall notify Client without undue delay, and in any event within
seventy-two (72) hours, of becoming aware of any personal data breach.

7. LIABILITY AND FORCE MAJEURE

7.1 Except in respect of death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud,
or breach of Section 5, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or
in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client
in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the
claim.

7.2 Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in
performing its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such failure
or delay results from an event beyond its reasonable control, including acts of
God, war, terrorism, epidemic, industrial action, or failure of a utility or
telecommunications provider. The affected party shall notify the other party
within ten (10) business days and shall use reasonable endeavours to resume
performance.

7.3 If a Force Majeure event continues for more than sixty (60) consecutive
days, either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice without
liability.
===PAGE===
8. TERMINATION

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if
the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty
(30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

8.2 On termination, Supplier shall return or securely destroy all Client data
within sixty (60) days and shall certify such destruction in writing.

9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the parties
submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be sent to legal@northwind-analytics.example
for Supplier and to contracts@cedarpoint.example for Client, and are deemed
received on the next business day following transmission.
